# How Far I've Gotten

This is a working document. I update it weekly. The version you're reading was last touched on a Sunday evening.

## Headline numbers

- 412 sign-ups since launch (eleven weeks ago)
- 117 of those have used the product more than once
- 41 have used it more than ten times
- 12 have paid me money for it
- 8 are still paying me money for it

I am not going to round any of these up. They are what they are. The conversion from sign-up to "paying me money" is approximately three percent, which is bad on most leaderboards but, importantly, non-zero. The conversion from "paying me money in week one" to "still paying me money in week four" is approximately sixty-seven percent, which is the number I actually care about.

## Revenue

I am earning, on average, about $640 a month from Lighthouse. This is genuinely not very much money. It is, however, more money than I was earning from Lighthouse three months ago, which was zero. The slope is the point. The intercept is, frankly, embarrassing.

If you want a forecast, I will tell you my forecast and I will tell you my forecast is mostly vibes. I think I can get to $5K MRR within six months of the raise, $20K MRR within twelve. I have a spreadsheet. The spreadsheet is in this data room. The spreadsheet has assumptions, and the assumptions are flagged.

## Product progress

The core flow has been stable for about six weeks. The bulk import shipped four weeks ago. I am currently working on the mobile experience, which is the thing I am least excited about but the thing my best customers ask for most often.

I have done eighteen customer-discovery calls since launch. I have a Notion page of patterns. The patterns inform what I build next, in roughly that order, with one exception: every fourth thing I build is something I want personally. That's the deal I made with myself.

## Things I tried that didn't work

- A pricing experiment with three tiers. I have one tier now.
- A landing-page rewrite that emphasised "AI". Conversion went down. I rewrote it without the word "AI". Conversion went back up. I have notes.
- A free trial of fourteen days. Conversion to paid was lower than a free trial of seven days. I have a theory about this. The theory is in the investment memo.

## Things I haven't tried

- Cold outbound. I know I should. I have not.
- A community. People keep telling me I should start one. I am not yet convinced this is a good use of the only employee's time.
- Paid acquisition. The product economics don't yet support paid acquisition. Once they do, I will.
