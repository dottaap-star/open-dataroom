# The Ask

I am raising **$1,000,000** at a **$10,000,000 post-money valuation**, in the form of a standard SAFE. I will accept cheques as small as $25,000 and as large as $250,000. I would prefer eight to ten investors over one or two.

## Why this size

I have done a budget. The budget is in the data room under "Progress". The short version is: $1M gets me eighteen months of runway at my current burn (which is mostly just my salary plus infrastructure), plus the budget to hire one senior engineer in month seven, plus a small marketing experiment.

If I raise more, I will be tempted to hire faster, and I think hiring faster than this would slow Lighthouse down rather than speed it up. The product has a clear shape and that shape is best maintained by a small number of people who understand it deeply.

## What I'd like investors to do

Three things, in this order:

1. Make introductions to customers who fit the ICP. I'll send you a one-pager on the ICP after the cheque clears.
2. Make introductions to one senior engineer who would consider a high-equity, modest-cash first-hire role. I have a profile.
3. Stay quiet, mostly. I will send a thoughtful, honest update once a quarter. I will ask for help when I need it.

I am specifically not raising for board members. I do not plan to have a board until I raise a real Series A. If you are looking for a board seat, we should pass on this round.

## Why now

The product works. It has paying customers, retention that I am not embarrassed by, and a clear next set of features. The reason to raise now is to remove the constraint of "I have a day job" and to bring on one engineer who can take over the work I'm not as good at.

Without this round, Lighthouse continues, but more slowly. With this round, Lighthouse becomes a thing I can credibly devote myself to full-time for the next eighteen months.

## Terms

Standard SAFE. Most-favoured-nation clause. No pro-rata for cheques under $50K. Pro-rata for cheques of $50K+. I have a template I am happy to share — it's the YC standard. I am open to amendments. I am not open to liquidation preferences.

## Closing

I would like to close this round within the next ninety days. I have so far had conversations with eleven investors. Five have soft-circled. I have included an updated pipeline in the data room.

If you've read this far, you already know whether you're interested. If you are, please send me an email — it's on the cover letter — and we'll find a time.

If you're not, that's genuinely fine. Thank you for reading. I'm sorry for the typos.
