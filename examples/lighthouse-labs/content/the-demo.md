# The Demo

There isn't a video. I considered making one, then I considered how long it would take to make one good, and I decided to spend that time fixing the bug that's been bothering me for three weeks instead. Sorry.

If you'd like to see the product, the best way is to open it in a browser. It runs at the URL on the cover page of the investment memo. The demo login is at the bottom of this file.

## What you'll see

The home screen is a single text input. You type the thing you want done, and the product does it. There is approximately one menu and three buttons. Some people have told me there should be more menus. Those people are wrong, but I have a list of their names and I'll revisit it once we have a thousand customers.

## What works

The core flow. End-to-end. From "I want to do the thing" to "the thing is done" in usually under twenty seconds, often under five. I have tested this on roughly eighty people. Eighty-two percent of them did the thing without asking me how.

The remaining eighteen percent had follow-up questions, almost all of which were about whether they could trust the output. That's a fair question and I will spare you my answer here — it's covered in the investment memo, in the section called "Trust", which is approximately three pages long and has footnotes.

## What doesn't work

A lot of things. Some highlights:

- The bulk import. It works but slowly. I know how to make it faster. I am going to make it faster as soon as the seed cheque clears.
- The mobile experience. It exists but it is not a thing I am proud of yet.
- The collaboration feature. It does not exist. People ask for it. I have a Notion page called "collaboration thinking" which is mostly questions I haven't answered yet.
