This assumes a $1M round at a $10M post-money cap. The numbers move with the cap. I'm open to discussing the cap. I am less open to discussing the round size — I think $1M is the right amount of money for what I'm trying to do in the next eighteen months, and raising more than that would put pressure on me to do things I don't currently want to do.

## On founder ownership

I think a single founder holding 73% post-seed is unusual. I think it's also right. I would rather own a controlling share of a thing I'm confident in than have raised more money and own less of it. If you'd prefer I optimise for the opposite, we are probably not a fit, and that's fine. I'd genuinely rather find that out now than later.

## Option pool

The 20% pool is unallocated. I do not currently plan to hire. If I do hire, the first three roles I'd hire for are listed in the investment memo, in the section called "The team I'd build if I had a team". The first hire is a senior engineer who can take over the parts of the product I am visibly not as good at.

## Vesting

My shares are vested. There is no vesting cliff. I am the sole employee and I built the product. I am happy to discuss a re-vesting agreement as part of the round if that's a standard request — most of my advisors say it isn't, for a single-founder pre-seed, but I'm not religious about it.
