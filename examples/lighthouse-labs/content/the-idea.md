# The Idea

I built Lighthouse because I got tired of how hard it was to do one specific thing, and then I noticed that hard-to-do specific thing kept coming up, and after the seventh time it came up I sat down on a Tuesday evening and started building.

The product is called Lighthouse. It solves a single, narrow problem and does almost nothing else. I am very serious about the "almost nothing else" part. If a feature isn't required to solve the core problem, it doesn't get built. I have a list of features I have not built — it is longer than the list of features I have built — and I review it every Sunday.

## The narrow problem

When you're trying to do *the thing*, you usually have to assemble five different tools, none of which were designed for it, and then you have to glue them together by hand and the glue breaks every few weeks. Lighthouse is the tool that was designed for it.

I'll spare you the longer pitch here. The investment memo has the longer pitch. This document is just so you know what the idea is, in plain English, before you read anything else.

## Why now

Three things are true now that weren't true two years ago:

1. The thing has become common enough that people are willing to pay to do it without the glue.
2. The underlying primitives are good enough that a single founder can build a credible v0 in a few weeks.
3. The bigger incumbents are focused on a different segment and aren't paying attention to this corner yet.

Number 3 won't last. That's part of why I'm raising now.

## Why me

I have done the thing professionally for six years. I know what's annoying about it from the inside. The first version of Lighthouse was a thing I built for myself. The fact that other people want it is, frankly, a bonus.

I am not the best engineer who could build this. I am, however, the founder most likely to actually ship the version that solves *my* problem in *my* way, and there is a not-trivial number of other people whose problem looks enough like mine that the same product works for them.
