# The Acme Catalog — Official Disclaimer

## Preamble

The Acme Capital catalog is a living document representing the current product lineup of Acme Industries Inc. ("Acme", "we", "us", "the Company"). It is updated quarterly, except when an anvil-related supply-chain event makes that materially impossible, in which case it is updated as soon as the involved parties have stopped falling.

By accessing the catalog, the investor acknowledges that they have read and understood this disclaimer in full, including the sections about combustibility, terminal velocity, and the relationship between Acme and the long-suffering Looney Tunes characters depicted within.

## Product Safety

All Acme products are sold "as-shipped" and are tested to a standard rigorously equivalent to "we tried it on Wile E. Coyote and most of him survived". The Company assumes no responsibility for misuse, including but not limited to: using anvils as breakfast tables, deploying dynamite as a smoke alarm, or attempting to operate rocket skates indoors.

The catalog is comprehensive but not exhaustive. Items not listed in the catalog do not exist in the Acme product line, and any reference to such items in subsequent fundraising materials should be treated as aspirational rather than indicative.

## Combustibility Notice (mandatory under Section 14B)

A non-trivial fraction of the catalog is, by design, combustible. The Company is in compliance with all applicable industrial standards, including but not limited to the relevant subsections of the Industrial Solutions Code of 1949 (last amended 2024).

Investors holding **Acme Premium** access tier are additionally cleared for the Top Secret Anvil Project ("TSAP"). TSAP-related documents are stored separately and require an additional confidentiality acknowledgement before review.

## Cohort Retention

Acme's customer cohorts exhibit unusually high churn, owing to the nature of the product line. The Company has nevertheless achieved stable revenue through aggressive cross-selling, the cohort-retention bonus afforded by the catalogue's annual relaunch cycle, and the inherent loyalty of customers whose previous order ended badly.

For full cohort metrics, see the **Dynamite Cohort Analysis** in the data room.

## Cap Table Note

Investors should note that "under active development" at Acme has a non-standard definition, encompassing items currently in production, items recently destroyed by a test run, and items the founders insist are real but have not been seen.
